Sort mail already in folders, and a tester that keeps looking

Two fixes to the folder editor. 1. "Test this rule" no longer stops at your 5 newest emails. It checks all the mail it would sort (the last 30 days, up to 200 emails, in Messages and every folder) and shows the ones that would go in, or says plainly that none would. 2. The save option becomes Where to look: Messages and each of your folders, each showing how many of its emails would go in the new folder. Check Must read and those emails move out of it when you save. The examples use a new School folder next to your Bank, Must read and Receipts. The mail is example data.

The test checks what the save would sort

One scan covers everything: every email in the window (30 days, up to 200) in Messages and all your folders, newest first, 20 at a time with progress, and you can stop it. It lists the ones that would go in, then the closest misses. The same results give the counts under Where to look. Mail you filed by hand is skipped, since it never moves.

Where to look, with counts

Replaces "Sort recent mail when I save". Each place shows how many of its emails would go in, e.g. Must read · 23 · 3 would go in. Messages is checked, as today. Folders stay unchecked until you check them: nothing leaves a folder without your say. The tester no longer suggests anything itself, since the counts are right there.

Saving reuses the test

If you tested and haven't changed the rule or the window, Create files exactly the emails the test found, with no second round of AI calls. Change either one and the counts show "Test again to count". Without a test, the save sorts as it does today.

Default window: 30 days, up to 200

Today it's 7 days and 100 messages, which misses most of what has piled up in Must read. 200 is also the most AI calls one test can make. You can still change both numbers. If nothing matches, the tester says how far back it looked, so you can widen it.

Editing a folder checks its own mail

When you change a folder's rule, that folder is checked under Where to look by default, and after a test its count reads "would leave": mail that no longer fits goes to another folder or back to Messages. After a sort, the message says how many moved and from where, with Undo.
