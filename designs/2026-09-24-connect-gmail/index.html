Connect Gmail

Connect Gmail and it joins your Busta mail in one list, newest first, as if it were one mailbox. Underneath, each account stays its own isolated mailbox. A color tells you which account a message belongs to: blue for jtaylorcpp@busta.app and teal for jtaylorcpp@gmail.com. Folders with the same name merge, and a rule can apply to both accounts or just one. Use the checkboxes in the account menu to choose which accounts you see. The Gmail messages are example data; your rules, folders and addresses are real.

Separate underneath, together on screen

Each account is its own mailbox (its own MailboxDO) in your org, with its own mail, folders and filing. The combined list asks every account you've selected for its newest mail and interleaves them by time. Nothing is copied between accounts, so disconnecting Gmail can't touch your busta.app mail.

Account colors

Blue for busta.app and teal for Gmail, shown as a thin stripe on each row, a dot on folders and a chip in threads. You can change them in Accounts. The picker leaves out red, amber, green and violet, because those already mean unread, in progress, success and the agent. Hues only ever appear as marks, never as row backgrounds.

Folders merge by name

"Must read" in both accounts shows as one folder with both dots and the unread counts added together. Matching ignores case and extra spaces. A folder only one account has (Gmail's "Receipts") shows just that account's dot. Opening a merged folder shows its mail from both accounts.

One rule, or one per account

A folder's editor has Applies to checkboxes. Checking both saves the same rule into each account, and each re-sorts its own mail. Unchecking one removes the folder from that account (its mail goes back to Messages). If the two accounts' rules are different, the editor shows both side by side, with "Use this one for both".

Hiding an account

Unchecking an account in the menu hides it from the list, folders, counts and search. It keeps receiving and filing mail. A bar says "Showing 1 of 2 accounts", and a dot on your avatar means a hidden account has unread mail. Your choice is saved to your account, so it's the same on your phone.

Which address you send from

Replies go from the account the email came to, so replying to Liz goes through Gmail. New mail defaults to the account you last sent from, and the From menu shows every account with its color. Gmail mail goes out through Google and is saved in Gmail's Sent.

Needs a yes

How far back

Connecting brings in the last 30 days of Gmail (7 days, 90 days and 1 year are the other choices), sorted by your rules as it arrives. That's one filing call per message, so for 1 year only the newest 1,000 are sorted. Spam isn't brought in, and drafts don't sync either way.

What stays in step with Gmail

Read, star, archive and trash go both ways, so the Gmail app on your phone agrees. Folders stay in Busta and Gmail labels are left alone. Busta can't permanently delete anything: Google's permission doesn't allow it.

The one-time Google setup

A Google Cloud project with the Gmail API, a sign-in screen set to In production (Testing signs you out every 7 days), and a Pub/Sub topic for new-mail alerts. You'll see "Google hasn't verified this app" once. I'll write the exact clicks, and the keys go in as Worker secrets.
